How a message becomes a score

01

Clean

Lowercase the text, then drop punctuation and common words such as “the” and “you”.

02

Stem

Loved and loving both become love, so the model treats them as one idea.

03

Weigh

TF-IDF turns those roots into numbers. Words that show up in every text count for less.

04

Score

Multinomial Naive Bayes adds the weights into a spam probability.

The study notebook compared several classifiers on this same dataset. This app uses Multinomial Naive Bayes on TF-IDF, the setup that favored precision when spam is the rare class.

Trained on the UCI SMS Spam Collection (Almeida, Hidalgo, and Yamakami, 2011). A demonstration project. It does not connect to a phone. The inbox check runs only when you press Run, and those messages are not saved.
